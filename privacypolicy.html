This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our wallet application ("App"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). 1. Information We Collect
1.1. Health Connect Data
Our app integrates with Health Connect by Android to read your step count (Steps) data after you grant explicit permission. We only access:
  • Steps data - total number of steps recorded per day.
We do not collect or access any other health metrics. The step data is retrieved locally from your device through Health Connect and is not stored permanently on our servers.
1.2. Other Data
We may collect limited technical or user-provided data, such as:
  • Email address (if you choose to register or subscribe for updates).
  • Basic analytics to understand app usage and improve functionality (non-personal, aggregated data only)
We do not collect your password, transaction history, device identifiers, or location data.
2. How We Use Health Connect Data
The Steps data accessed via Health Connect is used solely to:
  • Calculate and display your daily walking progress.
  • Reward you with in-app tokens (1 token per 1,000 steps, up to 10 tokens per day).
  • Maintain your daily streaks and motivate physical activity.
We use this data only within the app to provide the described functionality. We do not sell, share, or transfer your Health Connect data to any third parties.
2.1. Misuse and Fraud Prevention
In case any user is found to be manipulating, falsifying, or artificially inflating step data or rewards (including but not limited to using unauthorized apps, emulators, or spoofing tools), we reserve the right to restrict, suspend, or permanently block access to the wallet and associated rewards without prior notice.
3. Legal Basis (GDPR Compliance)
We process your data under the following legal bases:
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR): You explicitly grant permission via Health Connect for us to access your step data.
  • Legitimate Interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): To provide you with app features that enhance your experience and promote health-based rewards.
You may revoke your consent at any time via Health Connect settings on your device.
4. Data Storage and Security
  • Step data from Health Connect is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Any optional data you provide (e.g., email) is securely stored and protected using industry-standard encryption.
  • We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access or misuse.
5. Your Rights (Under GDPR)
You have the right to:
  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time via device or in-app settings.
  • Restrict or object to processing where applicable.
To exercise these rights, contact us at: support@thesharptoken.com.
6. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do not share or sell your Health Connect data or personal information with any third parties. The app may use anonymous, aggregated analytics (e.g., crash reports) that do not identify individual users.
7. Children's Privacy
Our app is not directed toward children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect legal or technical changes. The most current version will always be available at: https://docs.sharpeconomy.org/privacypolicy
9. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: support@thesharptoken.com.
Summary of Key Points
  • We only access Steps data from Health Connect, and only with your consent.
  • The data is used solely to calculate in-app rewards.
  • No health or personal data is ever shared or sold.
  • Fully compliant with Google Play's Health Connect policy and GDPR.
  • If users are found earning tokens falsely or using manipulative methods, their wallet access may be restricted or terminated.
