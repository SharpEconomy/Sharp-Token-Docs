This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our wallet application ("App"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (together, the "DPDP framework"), and U.S. state privacy laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA/CPRA).
For the purposes of the GDPR and the DPDP framework, the operator of the App is the data controller / Data Fiduciary responsible for your personal data. Our contact and grievance details are set out in the "Contact Us" and "Grievance Officer" sections below.
1. Information We Collect
1.1. Health Data (Health Connect on Android / HealthKit on iOS)
On Android, our app integrates with Health Connect by Android; on iOS, our app integrates with Apple HealthKit. In both cases, we read only your step count (Steps) data, and only after you grant explicit permission through the respective platform. We only access:
  • Steps data - total number of steps recorded per day.
We do not collect or access any other health metrics. The step data is retrieved locally from your device through Health Connect or HealthKit and is not stored permanently on our servers. We do not use Health Connect or HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and we never share or sell it to third parties. You can revoke this permission at any time via Health Connect settings (Android) or the Health app / Settings (iOS).
1.2. Account and Wallet Data
To create and operate your account and wallet, we collect and store:
  • Email address, and first and last name (if you register or subscribe for updates).
  • Account and wallet identifiers, and your authentication status (whether you are signed in).
1.3. Device, Technical and Diagnostic Data
Each time the App launches, it automatically collects and transmits technical information from your device to our servers. This includes:
  • Device identifiers - a unique device ID, device ID, and a device fingerprint used to recognise your device.
  • Device details - model, brand, manufacturer, type, and screen resolution.
  • Software details - operating system and version, SDK version, app version, build number, and install source.
  • Diagnostic and status data - battery level and charging state, total and available storage, total and available RAM, first-launch status, app install date, and last update date.
  • Preference and locale data - language, time zone (and time-zone offset), and user offset.
1.4. Network and Approximate Location Data
We collect your device's IP address, network type, and mobile carrier. From your IP address we derive an approximate location (country, region, and city). This is coarse, network-based location and is not GPS or precise geolocation; the App does not access your device's precise location.
1.5. Security and Fraud-Prevention Signals
To protect the integrity of rewards and prevent abuse, we collect signals indicating whether your device is rooted, whether it is running in an emulator, and the device fingerprint and unique device identifiers described above.
2. How We Use Your Data
2.1. Health Data (Health Connect / HealthKit)
The Steps data accessed via Health Connect (Android) or HealthKit (iOS) is used solely to:
  • Calculate and display your daily walking progress.
  • Reward you with in-app tokens (1 token per 1,000 steps, up to 10 tokens per day).
  • Maintain your daily streaks and motivate physical activity.
We use this data only within the app to provide the described functionality. We do not sell, share, or transfer your Health Connect or HealthKit data to any third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or any purpose other than the features described above. Our use of this data adheres to the Health Connect by Android Permissions policy (including its Limited Use requirements) and Apple's HealthKit guidelines.
2.2. Account, Device, Network and Diagnostic Data
We use the data described in sections 1.2 to 1.5 to:
  • Create, authenticate and maintain your account and wallet, and provide the App's core functionality.
  • Ensure the App runs correctly across devices, diagnose crashes, and improve performance, stability and features.
  • Detect, prevent and investigate fraud, manipulation of rewards, and other misuse (see section 2.3).
  • Provide security, apply appropriate language, time-zone and regional settings, and comply with our legal obligations.
  • With your consent where required, send you service updates and communications.
2.3. Misuse and Fraud Prevention
In case any user is found to be manipulating, falsifying, or artificially inflating step data or rewards (including but not limited to using unauthorized apps, emulators, rooted devices, or spoofing tools), we reserve the right to restrict, suspend, or permanently block access to the wallet and associated rewards without prior notice. We use root/emulator detection and device fingerprinting for this purpose.
3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR / DPDP)
We process your data under the following legal bases:
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; Section 4(1) DPDP Act): for accessing your step data via Health Connect (Android) or HealthKit (iOS) and for optional communications. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): to create and operate your account and wallet and deliver the rewards service you requested.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): to secure the App, prevent fraud and reward manipulation, and diagnose and improve performance, provided these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): to comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests.
Under the DPDP framework, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent or for legitimate uses permitted by law. You may withdraw consent as easily as it was given, via your device settings, in-app settings, or by contacting us.
4. Data Storage, Retention and Security
  • Step data from Health Connect or HealthKit is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Account, wallet, device, network, approximate-location and diagnostic data described in sections 1.2 to 1.5 is transmitted to and stored on our servers and those of our service providers, protected using industry-standard encryption and access controls.
  • We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law. When the purpose is served or you withdraw consent, we delete or anonymise the data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
  • In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify affected users and the relevant authority (including the Data Protection Board of India, where applicable) within the timelines required by law.
5. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do not sell your personal data for money. We share personal data only as follows:
  • Service providers / processors who host our infrastructure, provide analytics and crash reporting, and support fraud prevention, acting on our instructions under contract.
  • Legal and safety — where required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice as required by law.
We do not share or sell your Health Connect or HealthKit data with any third parties.
6. International Data Transfers
Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including outside the EU/UK and India. Where we transfer personal data of EU/UK users to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Transfers of data of Indian users are made in accordance with the DPDP framework.
7. Your Rights
Under the GDPR (EU/UK users), you have the right to:
  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to processing, and request data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time via device or in-app settings.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.
Under the DPDP framework (India), you have the right to:
  • Access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed.
  • Correct, complete, update, or erase your personal data.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Grievance redressal (see "Grievance Officer" below) and the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
Under U.S. state laws (e.g., CCPA/CPRA for California residents), you have the right to:
  • Know the categories of personal information we collect and how we use them, and access a copy.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information and limit the use of sensitive personal information.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.
Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: We do not sell your personal information for money. To exercise any right, or to opt out of any sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising, contact us at support@thesharptoken.com. We respond within the timelines required by applicable law (up to 90 days under the DPDP framework and within the periods required by GDPR and CCPA/CPRA).
8. Children's Privacy
Our App is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the age applicable in their jurisdiction — under 18 in India (as defined by the DPDP framework), under 16 in the EU/UK (subject to national law), and under 13 in the United States. Where the DPDP framework or local law applies, we require verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before processing a child's data, and we do not carry out behavioural tracking, monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
